About

We chose this topic because of our (indubitably) ever-so-enlightening teammate whom we shall not name pressuring us into it. Our truly brilliant teammate wanted to call out the 'glazing' and over-coverage of some renewable energies compared to others.

Besides peer pressure from our absolutely genius teammate, we also all found some modicum of interest in the topic at hand, leading us to commit it as our main exhibition topic.

We find the annual exhibitions to be a good way to display what we have learned not only in terms of research and implementation of topics but also in terms of creating, displaying, and presenting information.

We also wanted to do something out of the ordinary for our final year in the International Baccalaureate's Middle Years Program, the last year in which we could work as a team, and so we chose this relatively eccentric theme.
