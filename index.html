Basic Information

Our exhibition on the theme "Realities of Renewable Energy" talks about various topics relating to renewable energies including Solar, Wind, Tidal, Hydroelectric, Geothermal, Lightning, Wave, Hydrogen, Nuclear and Biomass. We explore some common renewable energies and some alternatives.

We explain how different renewable energies have drawbacks and benefits, as well as how different energies can be innovated and improved to be optimal for use in daily life, improving our lifestyles and environment, starting with energy.
